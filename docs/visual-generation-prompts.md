# Visual generation prompts

The shipped artwork is original local SVG source and does not require generated assets. If campaign variants are later produced, use these prompts as art direction—not as claims about shipped product behavior.

## Hero pipeline

“Original abstract vector illustration of a secure web-form pipeline: a browser card entering a shield-shaped validation gate, then branching to an inbox and webhook node; geometric, minimal, no logos, no people, deep navy with electric cyan and warm coral accents, transparent background, high contrast, accessible at small sizes.”

## Empty forms state

“Original minimal vector illustration of three floating form fields assembling into a tidy stack, a small plus symbol, rounded geometry, no text, no brand references, indigo and mint accent colors, works in both light and dark interfaces.”

## Security surface

“Original technical vector diagram of concentric permission rings around a workspace node, subtle key and audit-trail motifs, clean monoline geometry, no stock icon appearance, midnight blue and green palette.”

Do not generate testimonials, customer logos, usage charts, awards, or performance statistics without verified source data.
